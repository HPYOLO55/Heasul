Project: GlowUp AI – Your Personal AI Glow-Up Coach

Goal

Build a modern, premium Android-first web app called GlowUp AI. The app helps users improve their appearance and confidence using AI-powered image analysis and personalized daily guidance.

The application must be fully responsive, production-ready, and optimized for mobile devices.

- Primary Accent: Gold (#F5C542)

- Premium animations

- Fast loading
